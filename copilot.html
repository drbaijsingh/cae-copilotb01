Unit discipline. Neither ABAQUS nor LS-DYNA carries units — you do. Every script here is written in strict SI: metres, kilograms, seconds, so stress comes out in Pa and frequency in Hz. If you switch to millimetres you must switch mass to tonnes and density to t/mm³ (steel becomes 7.85e-9), or every natural frequency you compute will be wrong by a factor of about 31.6 and look plausible.
