How FGM is actually modelled. Neither ABAQUS nor ANSYS grades a property continuously through a shell. The standard approach — and what this generates — is to slice the thickness into layers, homogenize each one at its own mid-layer volume fraction, and build a composite section. More layers means a smoother gradation and a longer solve.

Reading the curve
Validation. The FGM routine returns the exact homogeneous plate rigidity at n = 0 and zns = 0, and converges on the metal at large n. The laminate routine reproduces A₁₁ = Eh/(1−ν²) and D₁₁ = Eh³/12(1−ν²) for a single isotropic ply, gives B = 0 and D₁₆ = D₂₆ = 0 for symmetric cross-ply, and returns λ = 19.7392 against the classical 19.739 for an isotropic square plate. Mori–Tanaka is bounded by Voigt above and Reuss below at every volume fraction. These are limiting-case checks, not a substitute for validating against your own FE model — which is what the generated scripts are for.
